## Color

- One dark theme: background near-black but not `#000` (e.g. `#0E0F11`), text off-white (e.g. `#E6E6E6`), surfaces rise in lightness by ~4% steps.
- Accents desaturated enough to avoid vibration on dark backgrounds; text contrast at least 7:1 for body copy.

## Rules for agents

- Do not add light-theme values or a theme switch; images and charts are prepared for a dark background.
