**Who it is for.** Products that connect people who offer something with people who want it, and earn on each transaction. Both sides are users with different needs, and trust between strangers is the product.

**What it adds.** Accounts and roles separate buyers from sellers. The catalog, uploads and favorites let sellers list and buyers find things. Checkout and payments move money, including the platform's share and seller payouts. Reviews and chat build trust, while abuse handling and the admin area let you resolve disputes and remove bad actors.

**Decide first.**
- Is it goods, services, rentals or digital items? That changes shipping, availability and what a review means.
- Who pays whom, when, and how does the platform take its fee? When are sellers paid out?
- Can someone be both buyer and seller with one account?
- Who decides in a dispute, and what can a buyer get back?
- How do you keep buyers and sellers from taking the deal off the platform?
