**Who it is for.** People who need answers from others: researchers, teams, event organizers, small businesses. The creators sign in, while the respondents often do not, so filling in a form must be effortless.

**What it adds.** Forms and surveys cover question types, logic and validation. The wizard module handles long forms split into steps with progress and saved drafts. Sharing sends a form out by link, and import/export brings questions and answers in and out of spreadsheets. Reports summarize results, notifications tell creators about new answers, and privacy covers consent and what happens to answers.

**Decide first.**
- Do respondents sign in, or are answers anonymous? Can one person answer twice?
- Which question types and conditional logic do you need on day one?
- Who can see the results, and can they be shared publicly?
- How long do you keep answers, and what do you owe respondents about their data?
- Is it free for creators, or limited by number of forms and answers?
