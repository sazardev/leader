**Who it is for.** Teams that need to see who is doing what and what is blocked: software, agencies, small companies. The same people open it many times a day, so it must be fast and quiet.

**What it adds.** Tasks and boards are the core: work items, statuses and owners. Workspaces and roles group people and projects, and comments, activity and notifications keep everyone aligned without meetings. Sharing opens a project to guests or clients, and reports show progress and workload.

**Decide first.**
- Is the unit a task list, a board with columns, or both? Can tasks have subtasks and dependencies?
- How is work organized: workspaces, projects, teams?
- Can people outside the team (clients, guests) see or edit work?
- Which updates should notify people, and which should stay silent?
- Do you need time tracking, deadlines or recurring tasks?
