**Who it is for.** Businesses that sell time or space: clinics, salons, studios, rentals, classes. Customers want to see what is free and book in a minute, and the business wants fewer no-shows.

**What it adds.** Calendar and booking handle availability, time zones and double-booking. Payments take a deposit or the full price, and scheduled jobs send reminders and release unpaid holds. Notifications keep both sides informed, and reviews build trust after the visit. Roles split customers from staff, and settings let the business set its own hours and rules.

**Decide first.**
- Who owns the calendar: one business, several staff, or several locations?
- Do customers pay upfront, a deposit, or on the day?
- What are the cancellation and rescheduling rules, and are there fees?
- How far ahead can someone book, and how long is a slot?
- Which reminders go out (email, SMS, push), and how long before?
