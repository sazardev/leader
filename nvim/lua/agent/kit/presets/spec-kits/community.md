**Who it is for.** Products where people are the content: a forum, a club, a fan space, a niche social network. Value grows with activity, and so does the risk of abuse.

**What it adds.** Accounts and onboarding turn visitors into members with a profile. Chat, comments and favorites let them talk and react, and activity and notifications pull them back. Abuse handling gives members and moderators tools against spam and harassment, while privacy and settings let people control who sees what.

**Decide first.**
- Is the community public, invite-only or split into private groups?
- What may people post (text, images, links, video), and what is forbidden?
- Who moderates, and what happens to a reported post or a blocked person?
- Do people use real names or pseudonyms?
- What minimum age do you accept, and how do you handle minors' data?
