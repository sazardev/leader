**Who it is for.** A business that sells its own products to the public and wants orders, stock and payments under its control. Shoppers expect a fast catalog and a checkout with no surprises.

**What it adds.** The catalog, inventory and favorites let people browse and keep track of what they want. Checkout, payments, coupons and invoices cover the purchase from cart to receipt. Reviews and notifications bring buyers back, and accounts keep their order history. States cover the empty, loading and error screens a shop meets constantly.

**Decide first.**
- Physical goods, digital goods or both? That decides shipping, stock and delivery.
- Can people buy without an account, and which payment methods do you accept?
- Which countries, currencies and taxes do you sell in?
- What is your return and refund policy, and who handles it?
- How is stock reserved while someone is paying?
