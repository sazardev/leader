**Who it is for.** Support and success teams that answer customers by email, chat or a form and must not lose a request. Agents want one queue and the customer's history in view.

**What it adds.** Support tickets hold each conversation, its status and its owner. CRM contacts give every ticket the customer's context. Notifications and sharing keep agents and customers informed and let a ticket be handed over, while reports show response and resolution times. Roles, SSO and the audit log suit a team that handles private customer data.

**Decide first.**
- Where do requests come from: email, a web form, chat, or all of them?
- How are tickets assigned (manual, round-robin, by topic), and what are the response targets?
- Can customers sign in to see their tickets, or do they only reply by email?
- Which customer data may agents see, and for how long do you keep it?
- Is there a knowledge base that should answer before a ticket is opened?
