**Who it is for.** Writers, editors and small publications that want to publish and grow an audience. Most visitors only read, a few write, and speed and search ranking decide whether anyone arrives.

**What it adds.** Content publishing covers drafts, review and scheduling. Media, tags and search keep a growing archive findable. The newsletter and sharing bring readers back and spread posts, while comments let them answer. Accounts and roles separate authors from editors, and privacy and localization cover consent and readers in other languages.

**Decide first.**
- Who writes: you, a team, or the readers? Is there an approval step before publishing?
- Is anything behind a paywall or for members only?
- Do readers need accounts, or only the newsletter and comments?
- Which languages will you publish in?
- Do you moderate comments before or after they appear?
