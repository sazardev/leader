# Audio credits

- **Keyboard** (`audio/keys/`): Holy Panda switch recordings from [kbsim](https://github.com/tplai/kbsim) by Thomas Lai,
  MIT License (`audio/keys/LICENSE.md`, kept with the files as the license asks). Also shipped by
  [Mechvibes](https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes) (MIT).
- **Music and effects**: original, synthesized by `audio.mjs` at render time (no samples). Free to use, no attribution.
- To use a track instead of the score, pass `--music file.mp3` or set `audio.music.file` in `story.js`. Free music
  with no attribution needed (CC0), by artists who publish under it themselves:
  - Loyalty Freak Music, e.g. "Minimal Ambient Bounce": https://archive.org/details/LoyaltyFreakMusic-minimalAmbientBounce
  - Komiku, e.g. "The Binge Watchers": https://archive.org/details/Komiku-the_binge_watchers
  - HoliznaCC0: https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0
