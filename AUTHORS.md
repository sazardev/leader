# Authors

Leader is created and maintained by **Omar Flores** ([@sazardev](https://github.com/sazardev)), with contributions from
the community.

## Contributors

Thanks to everyone who reported a bug, answered a question, tested a build, improved a guide or sent a patch. The
commit history and the release notes are the full record; if you contributed and want to be listed here by name, open a
pull request or a discussion.

## Built on

Leader stands on the shoulders of:

- [Neovim](https://neovim.io) and its Lua API;
- [NvChad](https://nvchad.com) for the base it grew from;
- [lazy.nvim](https://github.com/folke/lazy.nvim) and every plugin pinned in `nvim/lazy-lock.json`;
- [wgpu](https://wgpu.rs), [winit](https://github.com/rust-windowing/winit), [cosmic-text](https://github.com/pop-os/cosmic-text) and the Rust ecosystem behind the desktop app;
- the language servers, formatters, linters, parsers and model runtimes installed through Mason and the agent setup.

Leader is an independent project, not affiliated with the Neovim project, NvChad or any of the vendors of the backends
it can use.
